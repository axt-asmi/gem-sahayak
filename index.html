Surety Bonds on GeM.

An Insurance Surety Bond is a collateral-free instrument for meeting the Earnest Money Deposit (EMD) or security requirement of a GeM tender, accepted in place of cash deposits and bank guarantees.

Collateral-free
No margin money or pledged security
Three-step process
Completed online on GeM
IRDAI-licensed
Issued by regulated insurers
The three parties
I - Who obtains it
Principal
The contractor or bidder who obtains the bond.
II - Who requires it
Obligee
The government department or buyer that requires the security.
III - Who guarantees it
Surety
The IRDAI-licensed insurer that issues the bond and guarantees performance.
Distinction from a bank guarantee
No collateral or margin money is required.
Existing bank credit limits remain unaffected.
Working capital remains available for project execution.

Obtaining a surety bond on GeM

The procedure is completed online in three steps. The walkthrough and accompanying presentation are provided below for reference.

The three steps

1
Select and apply
Select the Insurance Surety Bond as the EMD on the GeM bid. GeM verifies the profile and rating, and may request GST or PAN validation.
2
Obtain a quote and pay
Insurers submit their quotes. Select the preferred quote and complete the payment online.
3
e-Sign and receive
Sign the documents, confirm the details, and the bond is issued.
